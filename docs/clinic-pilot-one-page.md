# Ventricura clinic pilot proposal

## Purpose

Run a small, clinician-supervised pilot of the Ventricura telehealth workflow: booking, secure appointment access, video consultation, clinician notes, and camera-derived measurement display.

## Proposed scope

- **Clinic:** [clinic name]
- **Pilot lead:** [clinician name]
- **Duration:** [for example, 4 weeks]
- **Participants:** [for example, up to 20 consenting adults]
- **Setting:** clinician-supervised telehealth consultations or scheduled test appointments
- **Comparator:** standard clinical observation equipment remains available and is used alongside Ventricura where readings are compared.

## What participants do

1. Receive a secure appointment link by email.
2. Read and accept the appointment consent prompt.
3. Join a video consultation using the browser.
4. Complete a clinician-directed camera check when requested.

## What clinicians can do

- create, reschedule and delete appointments;
- revoke and regenerate patient links;
- view a patient’s appointment and measurement history;
- create private clinical notes; and
- export the appointment measurement record.

## Information collected

Patient contact details, booking notes, appointment time, consent record, clinician notes, and saved measurement outputs. The clinic and Ventricura agree the permitted use, retention period and authorised access before the pilot starts.

## Safety and escalation

The clinician remains responsible for the consultation and uses established clinic processes and standard equipment whenever required. Any technical issue, unexpected result, access issue, or privacy concern is recorded and escalated to the pilot lead and Ventricura support.

## Pilot success measures

- secure link and email delivery success;
- successful appointment joining rate;
- technical reliability of video and camera capture;
- clinician usability feedback;
- comparison data against the agreed reference process; and
- privacy/security incidents and their resolution.

## Approvals before starting

The clinic’s governance, privacy and ethics contact confirms the pilot’s approved scope, participant materials, data handling, incident process, and any local requirements.
